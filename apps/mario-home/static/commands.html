What you can say to Mario

Say “Hey Mario” or press Super+J, then any of these. Other wordings usually work too; anything that isn't a command, Mario answers.

Gestures (with Air on, Super+G): open palm to take control · pinch to click · wave up or down to scroll · an L opened or closed to zoom · fist to grab a window · swipe for the next desktop · two open palms apart for every window.
