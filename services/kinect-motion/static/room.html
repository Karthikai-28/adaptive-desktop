In the room

Tracking coverage

Walk slowly around the area you use, alone, for a minute. Green cells: the Kinect saw your whole body there; red: parts were hidden or out of view. Move the Kinect or furniture until where you exercise is green.

Zones

Metres. x: left (−) and right (+) of the Kinect as you face the TV is mirrored on the map; z: distance from the TV; y: height on the wall. Stand where you want a zone and read your position above.

Events

Live at ws://127.0.0.1:8793/events as small JSON messages (room_occupied, person_enter_zone, raise_hand, point_at_zone, sit_reminder, exercise_rep…). To send them to Home Assistant, put its webhook URL in ~/.config/adaptive-desktop/motion.json as "webhook". Nothing leaves this computer otherwise.
